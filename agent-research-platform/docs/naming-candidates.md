# Naming Candidates

Curated name brainstorm for this platform (originally drafted for its Deno-era predecessor, "LLM Hill Climbing"; migrated from the stale yak-shears-py checkout). Relevant when the project graduates to its own repo and needs a real name. Themes: iterative improvement, branching, evaluation, optimization, non-deterministic microservices (ANDMs), lifecycle testing, and exploratory testing. None were selected at the time; per the original notes, variations on Bench/Branch felt closest but nothing landed.

## AI-Focused Names

- **IterAIte**: Iterative AI improvement.
- **AscendAI**: AI-driven ascent.
- **AIpex**: AI at the peak.
- **GradAIent**: Gradient-inspired AI.
- **AIscend**: AI-powered ascent.

## Non-English Inspired Names

- **Altiora** (Latin): "Higher" or "loftier."
- **Clivora** (Latin-inspired): Slope and ascent.
- **Eminar** (Latin "eminere"): To stand out or rise above.
- **Gradivo** (Italian "gradivo"): Progressing or advancing.
- **Skopos** (Greek): Aim, target, or focus.

## Pseudo-Realistic Made-Up Words

- **Evalion**: Evaluation meets innovation.
- **Optivus**: Optimized and purposeful.
- **Ascendra**: Evoking ascent and progress.
- **Varion**: Variants and iteration.
- **Iteron**: Iterative and futuristic.

## Unusual English Words

- **Echelon**: A level or rank in progress.
- **Pinnacle**: The highest point of achievement.
- **Conflux**: A merging of paths or ideas.
- **Verdict**: A decisive judgment.
- **Trajectory**: A path or progression.
