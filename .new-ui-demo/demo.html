Shaping A Fast Notes Surface

Apr 4, 2025 • 7 min read • Draft

We explore how minimal affordances, subtle motion, and careful type scaling create a calm space for capture and later synthesis.

Modern knowledge tools often drown intent beneath chrome: toolbars, nested menus, persistent side panels. This surface goes the other way—progressive disclosure, invisible defaults, and structural clarity surfaced only when momentum slows.

Interaction density is evaluated on a per-breakpoint basis. On an iPhone 14 viewport everything collapses into a single vertical flow. Larger screens layer preview and search affordances to support multi-window cognition.

Typography As Interface

Instead of decorative UI separators we rely on semantic whitespace, scale, and weight shifts. A fluid clamp scale prevents oversized jumps while preserving a feeling of editorial voice. Code, quoted context, and callouts adopt quiet contrast so prose remains lead.

# slug extraction for note URLs
def slugify(title: str) -> str:
    cleaned = title.lower().strip().replace(" ", "-")
    return ''.join(ch for ch in cleaned if ch.isalnum() or ch == '-')

Notice the shallow contrast of comments and the chroma accents for language tokens—distinct yet restrained.

Mobile First, Then Enhance

Baseline experience loads without scripting: readable text, accessible forms, navigable structure. HTMX augments hot paths: incremental search, inline preview, optimistic saves.

Calm software respects temporal context—it stays quiet when you are flowing and becomes gently helpful when you pause.

Focus & Motion

Focus rings are consistent: a soft halo with sufficient inner edge contrast. Motion curves share a familial feel—easing values tuned to feel physical but not bouncy. Reduced motion preference zeroes animations without collapsing layout.
